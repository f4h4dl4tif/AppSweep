How to install AppSweep
=======================

1. Drag AppSweep onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open AppSweep from Applications. macOS will say it "could not verify AppSweep
   is free of malware". This happens because AppSweep isn't notarized by Apple.
   Click "Done" (don't move it to the Bin).

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the Security
   section, and click "Open Anyway" next to AppSweep. Enter your password and
   click "Open Anyway" again. You only need to do this once.

   Alternative (Terminal), after copying AppSweep to Applications:
       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AppSweep.app

4. When AppSweep opens, follow its setup screen to turn on Full Disk Access and
   App Management, then click Relaunch.

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
