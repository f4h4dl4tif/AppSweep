AppSweep is installed

Opening it the first time: AppSweep isn't from the App Store, so macOS may say it can't verify the developer. If so, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to AppSweep. You only need to do this once.

Permissions: when AppSweep opens, it walks you through turning on two switches in System Settings:

  • Full Disk Access: so it can see and remove app data in protected folders
  • App Management: so it can delete apps from the Applications folder

Removed items go to the Trash unless you choose Delete Permanently.
